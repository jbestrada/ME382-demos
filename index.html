Crystallographic planes

Rotate cubic, hexagonal close-packed, and diamond-cubic unit cells to explore how crystallographic planes cut through their lattices.

Choose a crystal structure and its plane indices to see a plane intersect the unit cell. Atoms are drawn as touching hard spheres; turn on plane sections to highlight the circular areas cut through them. The diamond-cubic structure is tetrahedrally coordinated, not close-packed.

Structure and plane

The page reports whether the selected plane contains atom centers from both species. Binary default planes are mixed-species planes, not necessarily the densest or close-packed planes; those can be single-species layers.

Cubic structures use Miller indices (h k l).
